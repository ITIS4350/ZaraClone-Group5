Frequently Asked Questions

You can track your package directly through your order history in your ZARA account or via the tracking link sent to your confirmation email.
Items can be returned within 30 days from the shipment date of your order. All items must remain unworn and retain original tags.
Orders enter processing quickly. You can cancel within the first 15 minutes through your account details under Purchases.
We accept major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, PayPal, and ZARA Gift Cards.
